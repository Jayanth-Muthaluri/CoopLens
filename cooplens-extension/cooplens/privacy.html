Privacy Policy

CoopLens Chrome Extension · Last updated: June 2025

What CoopLens collects

CoopLens collects only what is necessary to analyze job postings:

  • Job description text scraped from the active browser tab when you click "Analyze my fit"
  • Your resume/profile text, which you paste manually in Settings
  • Your Groq API key, stored locally in Chrome's storage

Where your data goes

  • Your device only: Your API key, profile, and saved applications are stored in Chrome's local storage. They never leave your device except as described below.
  • Groq API: When you click "Analyze my fit", the job description and your profile are sent to Groq's API (api.groq.com) to generate the analysis. Groq's privacy policy applies to this data: groq.com/privacy-policy

What CoopLens does NOT do

  • Does not collect browsing history
  • Does not transmit data to any CoopLens-operated server
  • Does not use analytics or tracking
  • Does not share data with third parties beyond Groq (the AI provider you explicitly configure)
  • Does not store data remotely

Data deletion

All locally stored data can be deleted at any time via Settings → "Clear all data", or by removing the extension from Chrome.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
